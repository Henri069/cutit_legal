Delete your account and data

BikeEdit · Last updated: 29 July 2026

First things first BikeEdit has no user account in the usual sense. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. On first launch your device simply receives a random, anonymous ID. You can delete that ID and everything attached to it directly in the app, in under a minute, without asking us.

Option 1: delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open BikeEdit.
  2. Go to your video collection ("Tresor").
  3. Scroll to the bottom.
  4. Tap Alle Daten löschen (Delete all data).
  5. Confirm with Alles löschen (Delete everything).

To remove a single video, tap it in the collection and choose Löschen (Delete) there.

What gets deleted

Data What happens
All finished videos (MP4) Removed from storage immediately and permanently
All database records about your videos Deleted, including title, template and timestamps
The link to your anonymous ID Nothing is left that could be attached to it. The app behaves like a fresh install afterwards.
Render jobs still in progress Unfinished jobs expire after 24 hours at the latest

Deletion is final. There is no bin and no recovery. Videos you exported earlier to your gallery or to TikTok stay there, of course. You have to delete those in the respective app.

What is not deleted

  • Technical server logs with IP addresses and error codes. They contain no content and are overwritten shortly anyway. We keep them as long as needed for security and debugging.
  • Data we must retain for tax or commercial law reasons. This only applies to purchases, if you made any.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you already uninstalled the app, or cannot delete the data yourself for another reason, write to us:

henri.irmscher@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Subject: BikeEdit deletion request
  • Your anonymous ID from the app, if you still have it
  • Roughly when you used the app

We handle requests within 30 days. To be honest about the limits: because we deliberately do not know who you are, there is no name and no email address we could search by. Without the anonymous ID all we can confirm is that deleting the app removes the link on your device.

Do before publishing The app does not display the anonymous ID anywhere yet. For this route to actually work, it should be visible and copyable in the app, for example in small type under "Alle Daten löschen". There is also no server-side cleanup job yet for orphaned videos belonging to users who uninstalled without deleting first.

Uninstalling alone is not quite enough

If you simply uninstall BikeEdit, the anonymous ID disappears from your device. Your already rendered videos remain in private storage, with neither you nor us able to link them to anyone. So: first tap Alle Daten löschen in the app, then uninstall.
