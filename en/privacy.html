Privacy Policy

BikeEdit · Last updated: 29 July 2026

In short
  • No account, no email address, no password. On first launch BikeEdit only creates a random, anonymous ID for your device.
  • Your clips and your GPX route are processed for one purpose only: building your video.
  • Your finished video sits in private storage. Only your device can fetch it.
  • No ad tracking, no analytics SDKs, no selling of data.
  • You can delete everything yourself, from inside the app, at any time.

2. An anonymous ID instead of a user account

BikeEdit has no sign-up. On first launch the app signs in anonymously to our backend (Supabase anonymous sign-in). This creates a random ID that is stored on your device. It serves exactly one purpose: linking your uploaded clips and finished videos to your device so that nobody else can reach them.

We therefore learn neither your name nor your email address nor your phone number. The ID is not linked to any other service. Delete the app and the ID is gone from your device.

4. Location data in the GPX file

A GPX file contains the exact track of your ride, with coordinates and timestamps. That is sensitive data, so:

  • Uploading a GPX file is optional. BikeEdit works without one, just without map and speed overlays.
  • BikeEdit does not access your phone's location services and does not track you in the background. We only receive the file you pick yourself.
  • The route is used solely to draw the graphics in your video. It is not shared, not analysed and not compared against other users.
  • If you do not want your starting point (your home address, for example) visible in the video, trim the GPX file before uploading or pick a template without a map.

5. Access to photos and videos

To pick clips, BikeEdit opens your operating system's media picker.

  • Android: we use the system photo picker. The app gets no access to your whole gallery, only to the files you tap in the picker.
  • iOS: you are asked for photo library access. You can choose "Selected Photos" and decide yourself what BikeEdit may see.

We do not scan your gallery and we upload nothing you did not select.

6. Music

BikeEdit either embeds royalty-free or AI-generated music, or renders your video silent so you can add a song from TikTok's or Instagram's own catalogue. We never ship a protected commercial master. If you search for a song title in the app, that search term is sent to our music catalogue. It is not tied to any profile.

7. No tracking, no advertising

BikeEdit contains no analytics, advertising or attribution SDKs. There is no profiling, no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR and no sharing with ad networks. We do not sell data.

9. Security

  • All traffic between app and server is TLS encrypted.
  • Video storage is private. Access happens only through short-lived signed links.
  • On every request the server checks that the data really belongs to your ID. Ownership always comes from the verified auth token, never from a value the device sends.
  • Links to individual media files expire after one hour.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data (Art. 15)
  • Rectification (Art. 16)
  • Erasure (Art. 17)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18)
  • Data portability (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent with future effect (Art. 7(3))

The fastest way to erase your data is to do it yourself in the app. See Delete data. For anything else, write to henri.irmscher@gmail.com.

Important: because BikeEdit deliberately does not know who you are, we can only act on an email request if you include your anonymous ID. Where to find it is explained on the Delete data page. Without that ID we cannot locate your data and, for security reasons, cannot hand anything over.

You may also complain to a supervisory authority. Ours is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Postfach 3163, 65021 Wiesbaden, Germany.

11. Children

BikeEdit is not aimed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

12. Changes
