The problem

Thirty clips, none posted

Back from a ride, camera roll full, and nothing ever goes up. Editing takes too long, the music never lands on the cuts, and your route sits inside an app that has nothing to do with any of it.

Kadenz takes that part off your hands.

How it works

Four steps

  1. Pick your clips

    Photos and videos from the ride. Kadenz orders them by capture time so the ride tells itself in order.

  2. Choose a vibe

    HYPE, SMOOTH or RETRO. The vibe sets how hard the cuts land and which transitions show up.

  3. Pick your song

    From the included catalogue or your own favourite. Kadenz reads the tempo and cuts right on the beat.

  4. Share it

    A few minutes later your edit is ready. To your Story, Reels and TikTok, onto your Strava activity, or just saved.

Three vibes

The vibe does the cutting

One tap sets cut rhythm, transitions and colours. No sliders, no tracks, no timeline.

HYPE

Short cuts on the beat, clips interleaved. Twice as fast on the drop.

SMOOTH

Calm, clean, relaxed. Long shots and soft transitions.

RETRO

Hard cuts, split screen, stickers. Medium pace.

Your route

The ride comes along

Drop in your GPX file from Strava, Komoot or Garmin Connect. Kadenz draws the route into the video and adds distance, speed and climbing. Three styles to pick from.

  • COMET Bright head, glowing tail. The line travels with you.
  • CLIMB The colour shows the gradient. Where it hurt, you can see it.
  • PURE Thin, minimal, quiet. For videos that speak for themselves.

No GPX works too. You just get the video without the map and without the stats.
